# Pao Mo (羊肉泡馍)

Last Updated 12.25.2024

Serves two people.

## Ingredients

### Soup Base

* 1.5lb meat, diced into 1in cubes (stew-grade lamb / beef / less-fatty pork
  cuts)
* 6c stock + 4c water
* 1tsp fennel seeds[^1]
* 1tsp Sichuan peppers
* 4 star anise
* 1 stick cassia bark
* 3 dried Thai chilies
* 2in ginger, smashed
* 5 green onions, lightly smashed
* 1tsp salt

### Fixins

* two servings noodles (e.g. sweet potato etc.)
* 1/4c dried wood ear mushrooms
* 2 dried shiitake mushrooms
* half bunch cilantro, chopped
* 0.5lb wintermelon, sliced ~0.25in
* 1 recipe's worth of unleavened [bing](/mains/bing.md)

## Directions

### Soup Base

1. Cover meat in cold water and boil for 10min.
1. Skim off fat and rinse meat.
1. Add meat to stock and water and all spices except salt, and bring to a boil.
1. Simmer for 2hrs.

### Fixins

* Dried mushrooms should be rehydrated in hot water for ~1hr.
* Shiitake mushrooms should be diced.
* Wintermelon takes ~10min to cook.
* Noodles take ~15min to cook.

1. Add fixins to broth such that everything finishes at the same time.
1. Cilantro and optionally chili paste, sesame oil, vinegar etc. are garnishes.
