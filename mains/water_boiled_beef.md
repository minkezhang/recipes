# Water-Boiled Beef (水煮牛肉)

Last Updated 01.02.2026

Serves two - three people.

![shuizhu_beef](/assets/shuizhu_beef.jpg)

## Ingredients

* 9oz beef tenderloin or flank steak
* 1tbsp Shaoxing wine
* oil
* 2tsp Sichuan pepper (green)
* 10 - 12 dried chilies, halved and deseeded
* 1tbsp corn starch
* 3tbsp pixiandouban
* 2tsp ground red chiles
* 1tbsp garlic, minced
* 1tbsp ginger, minced
* 1 1/2c chicken stock
* 1/4tsp dark soy sauce (for color)
* two green onions, julienned (finishing)

### Vegetabls

#### Enoki and Soybean Sprouts

* 7oz enoki mushrooms, washed, drained, and trimmed
* 6oz soybean sprouts, washed and drained

#### Napa Cabbage and Celery

* 7oz Napa cabbage, sliced 2in by 0.5in
* 2 celery sticks, sliced roughly with the same dimensions

## Directions

### Marinade

1. Slice beef across grain into ~1/8in slices.
1. Mix with 1/4tsp salt, Shaoxing wine, and 1tbsp oil. Let sit.
1. Add 1tbsp corn starch and 1tbsp cold water to marinade and mix.

### Vegetables

1. Heat 3tbsp oil in wok over high heat.
1. Add chiles and stir for 15 - 30s (not yet blackened).
1. Add Sichuan peppers and stir for 30s.
1. Strain spices from oil, and return oil to medium heat.

#### Enoki and Soybean Sprouts

1. Stir-fry soybean sprouts for ~2min.
1. Add enoki mushrooms and stir-fry for ~2 - 3min until wilted.

#### Napa Cabbage and Celery

1. Stir-fry in wok for ~1 - 2min.
1. Add 1/4tsp salt.

1. Pour vegetables and oil into base of serving bowl (may need two bowls).

### Meat

1. Add 3tbsp oil to wok over medium heat.
1. Add pixiandouban and stir for ~30s until oil is red.
1. Add *ground* chiles (not the fried ones), stir, and add ginger and garlic for ~30s.
1. Add stock and soy sauce and bring to boil.
1. Add beef and separate.
1. Wait for stock to start boiling, and then wait for another 30s, then plate the stock and beef on top of the vegetables.

### Finish

1. *Clean* wok and add 5tbsp oil over high heat.
1. Add fried spices on top of meat.
1. Wait until oil is sizzling, then pour over dish.
1. Garnish with spring onions.
