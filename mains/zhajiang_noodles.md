# Zhajiang Noodles (f炸酱面)

Last Updated 12.05.2025

Serves two - three people.

## Ingredients

* 1lb noodles

### Lu (卤)

* 1/4c oil
* 2tsp Sichuan pepper
* 4 star anis
* 3 bay leaves
* 1 small cinnamon stick
* 10oz ground pork
* 4tbsp minced ginger
* 2tbsp Shaoxing wine
* 10tbsp tianmianjiang

### Toppings

* 1 celery stick
* 1 small carrot
* 1 cucumber
* raw or pickled garlic

## Directions

### Lu

1. Add oil over high flame and add Sichuan pepper, star anis, bay leaves, and
   cinnamon stick; heat for ~30s or until fragrant and remove from wok.
1. Add pork and stir-fry until pale.
1. Add ginger and stir-fry for ~30s or until fragrant.
1. Add Shaoxing wine, stir, and add tianmianjiang; stir-fry for ~1min.
1. Add ~1c water (until pork is covered by water); this will be reduced.
1. Bring to boil and then simmer over low heat for ~15min until thickened.

### Toppings

1. Julienne vegetables of choice.
1. Blanch vegetables if applicable (celery, carrot) for ~90s.

### Noodles

1. Cook noodles per instructions.
1. Reheat sauce[^1].
1. Serve with sauce, toppings, and minced (or whole) garlic.

### Reheating

1. Add sauce to wok and heat until same consistency as fresh.
