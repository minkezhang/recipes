# Bing (饼)

Last Updated 12.25.2024

Serves two people.

![bing](/assets/bing.jpg)

## Ingredients

### Leavened

* 2c all-purpose flour[^1]
* 3/4c water
* 1tbsp yeast mixed with a small amount of warm water

### Unleavened

* 2c all-purpose flour[^1]
* 2/3c warm water
* 1/2tsp baking powder

## Directions

### Leavened

1. Mix flour, yeast and water; add water gradually to prevent overwatering.
1. Cover and let rise 1hr.
1. Divide into two portions for two ~8in diameter _bing_.
1. Heat pan with some oil.
1. As with the unleavened _bing_, let cook (for ~7 - 8min) or until _bing_ rises
   quickly after depressed with finger.

### Unleavened

1. Mix flour and baking powder.
1. Incorporate water and knead.
1. Cover and let rise 30m.
1. Roll portions of dough out into ~1/3in thick. Dust.
1. In medium-high pan (_without_ oil), cook dough until browned. Each side takes ~2 - 3min.
   Flip every minute or so until done.

[^1]: Alternatively, use 1.5c all-purpose flour and 0.5c cornmeal.
