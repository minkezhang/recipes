# Vegetable Stir Fry

Last Updated 06.09.2026

Serves one person.

## Ingredients

* 1/2lb spinach[^1], rinsed and dried
* 2 tablespoons cooking oil
* 2 cloves minced / sliced garlic
* 1/2tsp salt
* 1/4tsp sugar
* 1/4tsp sesame seed oil
* 1tsp ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Rinse and dry spinach.
1. Heat oil on medium heat.
1. Add garlic and salt and cook until fragrant.
1. Add spinach and stir-fry for 1 - 2min until almost wilted.
1. Add sugar, sesame seed oil, pepper. Serve.

[^1]: Preferably substitute with pea tips.
