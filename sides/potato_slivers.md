# Potato Slivers (土豆丝 / 萝卜丝)

Last Updated 12.25.2024

Serves one person.

![potato slivers](/assets/potato_slivers.jpg)

## Ingredients

* 1/2lb potatoes or turnip, julienned ~3in
* 2tbsp oil
* 3 dried chilis, whole or sliced
* 2 green onions, sliced
* 1/4tsp ground Sichuan peppers
* 1 fresh pepper, e.g. jalapeno, seeded and sliced
* 1tbsp Chinese vinegar
* 2tsp light soy sauce
* 1/2tsp sugar
* 1/4tsp salt

## Directions

1. Rinse potatoes and soak in water until ready.
1. Drain and pat dry potatoes.
1. Heat wok on medium-high and add oil.
1. Add chilies and green onions and cook until fragrant.
1. Add Sichuan pepper and cook until fragrant.
1. Add potatoes and cook for ~1min.
1. Add fresh peppers and cook for ~30s.
1. Add vinegar, soy sauce, sugar, salt and cook for 1 - 2min. Potatoes should
   still be crispy, not soft. Serve.
