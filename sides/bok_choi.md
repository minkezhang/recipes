# Ginger Bok Choi with Oyster Sauce

Last Updated 12.05.2025

Serves one to two people.

## Ingredients

* 3/4lb baby bok choi
* 2tbsp oil
* 2tbsp ginger, julienned
* 1/4c water

### Sauce

1. 1tbsp cornstarch
1. 2tsp light soy sauce
1. 1tsp oyster sauce
1. 2tsp Shaoxing wine
1. 1tsp sesame oil
1. 1/4c chicken stock
1. pinch of salt
1. pinch of white pepper
1. pinch of MSG
1. 1tsp sugar

## Directions

1. Separate bok choi leaves and wash.
1. Mix cornstarch with all other sauce ingredients, adding the stock *after*
  cornstarch is already incorporated.
1. Heat oil over medium flame and add ginger for ~1min or until lightly golden.
1. Add bok choi and mix with oil for ~15s.
1. Add water and cover with lid; steam for ~45s.
1. Add sauce and toss for ~30s or until thickened. Do not overcook the bok choi.
1. Serve.
