# Vegetable Stir Fry

Last Updated 12.25.2024

Serves one person.

![vegetable stir fry](/assets/vegetable_stir_fry.jpg)

## Ingredients

* 1/2lb vegetables[^1]
* 4 garlic cloves
* 1tbsp Sichuan peppers
* 2 dried chilis, sliced
* 2tbsp cooking oil
* salt
* sesame seed oil

### With Eggs

* 1 - 2 eggs (optional), beaten w/ salt
* 1 green onion (optional)

## Directions

1. Cut vegetables into 1/4in slices or some other suitable shape.
1. Heat wok on medium-high and add oil.
1. Add Sichuan peppers and chilis and cook until fragrant.
1. Add garlic and cook until fragrant.
1. Add vegetables and cook for 1 - 2 minutes or until done (varies depending on
   vegetable).
1. Sprinkle salt and sesame seed oil (optional) in pan before plating.

### With Eggs

1. Reusing the same wok, add a bit of oil and scramble eggs.
1. Set aside and cook green onions until fragrant.
1. Add everything back into wok and stir. Serve.

[^1]: Spinach, green beans, celtuce stem, bitter melon, chayote, etc.
