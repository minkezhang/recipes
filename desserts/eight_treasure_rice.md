# Eight Treasure Rice (八宝饭)

Last Updated 02.15.2026

2 - 3 servings

## Ingredients

* 1c sweet / glutinous rice
* 1c black glutinous (forbidden) rice[^1]
* ~1tbsp mix of roasted, chopped nuts, e.g. walnuts, peanuts, cashews, lotus seeds, salted egg yolk
* ~1tbsp mix of sweet, diced fillings e.g. orange peels, kiwi, raisins, crystalized pineapples and ginger, etc.
* ~1tbsp mix of tart, diced fillings, e.g. cranberries, goji berries, etc.
* ~1tbsp dates, pitted and whole
* 1tbsp toasted sesame
* 1c red bean paste[^3]
* 4tbsp sugar
* 4tbsp coconut oil, lard, or butter
* salt
* osmanthus / chrysanthumum syrup

## Directions

1. Submerge rice in water and sit overnight.
1. Steam 1c rice and 1.25c water in a rice cooker.
1. Mix rice with 2tbsp sugar and 2tbsp coconut oil.
1. Repeat with black glutinous rice.
1. Oil bowls and sprinkle bottom with the sesame seeds.
1. Put in a single layer of nuts and fruits at the bottom of the bowl.[^2]
1. Spread black rice over the filling.
1. Add red bean paste over rice, with room at the edges.
1. Spread rest of the rice on top, making sure to fully envelope the red bean
   paste.
1. Steam for 30min.
1. Flip and drizzle with syrup. Serve.

### Reheating

1. Steam for ~10min.

[^1]: Or just use another 1c glutinous rice.
[^2]: Dates can be arranged into a floral pattern by cutting the dates into quarters -- see source for pattern.
[^3]: Or lotus seed, taro, pumpkin, chestnut, or black sesame paste, etc.
