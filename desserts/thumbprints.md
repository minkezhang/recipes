# Thumbprints

Last Updated 05.05.2026

Makes ~40 cookies.

![thumbprints](/assets/thumbprints.jpg)

## Ingredients

### Dough

* 1c crunchy peanut butter, stirred
* 1/2c unsalted butter, softened[^1]
* 1/2c white sugar
* 1/2c brown sugar
* 1 egg, beaten, room temperature
* 1tbsp vanilla extract
* 1.5c flour
* 1tsp baking powder
* 1tsp cornstarch
* 1/4tsp salt
* 1/2c white sugar[^2]
* 2tsp ground cinnamon[^2]

### Filling

**N.B.**: Fillings are scaled up for a full batch of thumbprints *each*.

#### Marmalade

* 1/2c orange marmalade
* 2tbsp ginger, minced

#### Raspberry

* 1/2c raspberry preserves

## Directions

### Dough

1. Cream peanut butter, butter, sugar, and brown sugar.
1. Add egg and vanilla extract; mix.
1. Mix and sift the flour, baking powder, and corn starch.
1. Stir in dry ingredients into batter.
1. Line trays with parchment paper and scoop out ~1tbsp of dough and shape into
   a ball.
1. Dust with the excess sugar / cinnamon and press indent.
1. Chill in freezer for 30min.

### Marmalade

1. Microwave for ~10s.
1. Sprinkle ginger on top of each assembled thumbprint. Optionally, replace with
   candied ginger.

### Raspberry

1. Microwave for ~10s.

### Thumbprints

1. Take out of freezer.
1. For each thumbprint, add enough filling to level off the indent.
1. Preheat oven to 375F.
1. Bake for 10min.
1. Rest until completely cooled.

[^1]: Try adding another 1/8c butter next time, as dough turned out a little
    crumbly.
[^2]: This sugar is for dusting the dough, and not used in the batter itself.
    Optional.
