# Peach Cobbler

Last Updated 06.09.2026

Serves four people.

## Ingredients

* 1c all-purpose flour
* 1tsp baking powder
* 1/2tsp salt
* 1/4tsp baking soda
* 1/2c granulated sugar
* 2tbsp brown sugar
* 1/2c butter, divided and melted

### Filling

* 45oz canned peaches in syrup

*or*

* 3c peaches, peeled and diced (~3 large peaches)

### Syrup

* 1.5c [ginger syrup](../spices/ginger_syrup.md)[^1]
* 0.5c lemon juice

## Directions

1. Marinate peaches in syrup overnight.
1. Preheat oven to 350F.
1. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, baking soda, granulated sugar.
1. Add 1/4c melted butter and stir.
1. Layer 1/2 of the flour mix onto the bottom of a 9x9 baking pan.[^2]
1. Add marinated peaches and 1c of ginger syrup liquid.
1. Layer remaining dry mix on top.
1. Sprinkle on the brown sugar on top.
1. Bake for 50min or until the center of the pan is bubbling.
1. Let sit for 5min.
1. Serve with ice creamm.

[^1]: Use 1:2 sugar to water instead of the usual 1:1. Avoid cloying sweetness.
[^2]: Double servings can be made in a 9x13 instead.
