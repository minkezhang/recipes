# Peach Cobbler

Last Updated 06.09.2026

Serves four people.

## Ingredients

* 1c all-purpose flour
* 1tsp baking powder
* 1/2tsp salt
* 1/4tsp baking soda
* 3/4c granulated sugar
* 2tbsp brown sugar
* 1/2c butter, divided and melted
* 29oz canned peaches in syrup

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350F.
1. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, baking soda, granulated sugar.
1. Add 1/4c melted butter and stir.
1. Layer 1/3 of the flour mix onto the bottom of a 9x9 baking pan.[^1]
1. Add peaches and syrup.[^2]
1. Serve with ice cream.

[^1]: Double servings can be made in a 9x13 instead.
[^2]: Also consider replacing / reusing syrup with 1c of [ginger syrup](../spices/ginger_syrup.md). If adding tea, add in a cheese cloth and let sit for ~5min after turning off heat and remove. Do not squeeze the cloth when removing.
