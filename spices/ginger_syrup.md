# Simple Syrup

Last Updated 12.05.2025

Makes 1c syrup. Keeps for ~1w.

## Ingredients

1. 1c dark brown sugar, packed
1. 1c ginger, sliced
1. 3/4c water
1. 2 cinnamon sticks
1. 1 dried chili
1. 1tsp vanilla
1. orange peel
1. pinch of salt
1. pinch of cloves

## Directions

1. Add sugar and water to pan on medium flame and stir until dissolved.
1. Add ginger and everything else and bring to light boil.
1. Cover and reduce heat; simmer for ~15min.
1. Remove from heat and take out orange peels and chili; steep for ~1hr.
1. Strain and fridge.
