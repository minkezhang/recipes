# Egg Drop

Last Updated 04.14.2026

Serves 2 - 3 people.

## Ingredients

* 1lb potatoes
* 1lb tomatoes
* 3 eggs, lightly beaten
* 2 - 3 green onions, diced
* 4c chicken stock
* dash of sesame seed oil
* pinch of white pepper
* pinch of MSG
* pinch of salt

## Directions

1. Peel and chop potatoes into 1in chunks.
1. Blanch, peel, and chop (or slice) tomatoes into 0.5in chunks.
1. Add a pinch of salt to eggs.
1. Add stock, sesame seed oil, white pepper, MSG, and salt into pot.
2. Bring to boil and add potatoes.
1. Let simmer for 15 - 20min.
1. Add tomatoes and let simmer for 1 - 2min.
1. Add cornstarch slurry and stir.
1. Slowly add eggs and stir.
1. Serve with green onions as garnish.
